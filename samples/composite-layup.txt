Wet layup is the simplest way to build a carbon fiber composite part. Dry fabric is placed on a mold, saturated with epoxy resin, and the next ply is added on top. Alternating fiber orientations such as 0, 45 and 90 degrees spreads stiffness across several load directions. Vacuum bagging then pulls out trapped air and excess resin, which raises the fiber volume fraction and makes the part stronger and lighter.
