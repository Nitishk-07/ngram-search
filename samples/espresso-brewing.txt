Espresso is brewed by forcing hot water at about nine bars of pressure through a tightly packed puck of finely ground coffee. A typical shot takes 25 to 30 seconds and produces around 36 grams of liquid from 18 grams of grounds. Grind size matters most: too coarse and the shot runs fast and sour, too fine and it chokes the machine and tastes bitter. The golden foam on top is called crema.
