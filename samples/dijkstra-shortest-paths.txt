Dijkstra's algorithm computes the shortest path from a single source vertex to every other vertex in a graph with non-negative edge weights. It keeps a priority queue of vertices ordered by their tentative distance. Each step removes the closest unvisited vertex, marks its distance as final, and relaxes the edges leaving it. With a binary heap the algorithm runs in O((V + E) log V) time. Navigation apps, network routers and game AI all rely on variations of this idea.
